FAQs

Everything you need to know before you paddle into Thailand's extraordinary seascapes.

John Gray's Sea Canoe offers award-winning tours through stunning sea caves, hidden lagoons and breathtaking island scenery. With experienced guides and a genuine eco-friendly approach, it's one of the most distinctive ways to explore Thailand's natural beauty.

Our eco tours are built around education, conservation and authentic connection with the natural world, while aiming to minimise environmental impact and support local traditions and communities.
