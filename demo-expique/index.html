Experience Unique Bangkok With ExpiQue

We love Bangkok! We Live Bangkok! ExpiQue is a boutique and innovative tour operator, with a passion for showing guests the uniqueness and beauty of Bangkok and the Thai Culture. We love Thai food, street food, local markets, sharing stories of local people and culture, tuk tuk rides, exploring in unique ways ....

We provide more than just a tour guide and a checklist of Bangkok's highlights (e.g. The Grand Palace, Wat Pho, Chinatown,...). We provide an insight in to local life, the street food scene, something off the beaten path.

We focus on offering a selection of tours and experiences which can stand out as truly memorable. These include our range of award winning night tours by tuk tuk. In addition we offer selection of walking tours and food tours. So discover more when visiting Bangkok on a tour with us.
